# Northwind Labs — internal brief

Anita Rao is the head of engineering at Northwind Labs. She leads Project Atlas,
the company's main data platform.

Project Atlas runs on the Kestrel storage engine. Kestrel handles all of the
write traffic for Atlas.

Kestrel was originally built by a small team at Fenwick Systems, led by Ben Carter.
Ben Carter is now a principal engineer at Northwind Labs.

Northwind Labs acquired Fenwick Systems in March 2024. The acquisition brought
the whole Kestrel team into Northwind.

Diana Lopez is the chief financial officer of Northwind Labs. She approved the
Fenwick acquisition and now sponsors the Atlas budget.

Chen Wei manages the Singapore office of Northwind Labs. The Singapore office
runs the support desk for Project Atlas customers.
